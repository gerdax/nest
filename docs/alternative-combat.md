# Alternative rat encounter

Open [the alternative rat example](../player.html?example=rat) with `python3 serve.py`. It creates a fresh, non-saving project and starts directly at the encounter. [The classic example](../player.html?example=rat-classic) retains its Weapon pickup and original combat behavior. Neither example replaces the saved project. Original prototypes and artwork remain preserved.

This example follows the 31 October email brief. Its Enemy opts into the alternative rules through `mechanics: 'opposed-preparation'`; ordinary combat continues to use the existing rules. The runtime coordinator selects the rat's declaration before the player decides. The rat either bites or prepares; preparation conceals whether it intends to defend or charge, displaying “The rat prepares…”. The declaration remains fixed for that decision.

The player has three Common Actions, with no Weapons or pickup:

| Action | Resolution | Result | Cost |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Attack | Agility, difficulty 3 (currently 3d6 + 3 ≥ 13) | 2 damage before defense | None |
| Defend | Direct | Guaranteed full block; no defense roll | 1 Energy, paid when the card is played |
| Preparation | Direct | Next action only: Attack gains 2 damage, or breaks defence for base damage only | None |

The rat's Bite also checks 3d6 ≥ 10 and deals 2 damage. Its Prepare action supplies 4 damage reduction and a preparation bonus of 2 for the coordinator's declared defense or charge. A charge deals 4 damage. Player Defend costs 1 Energy up front and guarantees a full block of an incoming attack, including a charge, without a defense roll. When an attack hits and is blocked, the Defend action’s **Block reveal** card appears (default: “You blocked the attack”). Swipe it to return to player choices. Misses skip this card. The feedback is a separate shared card in **Common → Effects Feedback → Dodge**, alongside **Damage**. Edit its description, artwork and transforms there. **Defend → Reveal**, directly below **Image scale and position**, selects the feedback card and links to its definition; Weapon Defend Actions have the same controls. Dodge uses the Effect layout with no damage number or resource icon. Existing embedded reveals are copied into shared Feedback definitions with their authored copy and artwork preserved. The reveal is presentation only and applies no effects. The rat still checks its own defense after an ordinary player Attack hits. A prepared player Attack breaks a rat block without a defensive roll, loses its preparation damage bonus and deals its rolled base damage. It still needs to hit. Rat defense results use `SUCCESS` or `FAIL`; missed attacks and charges retain `MISS`. Defend and Prepare cards are direct actions. Existing guard timing remains: a missed incoming attack or rat hold consumes the defense. In the saved `rat-alternative.json` project, Punch is free and retains its authored 1–2 base damage; the non-saving example above retains its 2 damage.

Under **Library → Enemy → Combat Actions**, **Action selection** chooses **Random** or **Order**. Random uses each normal action’s **Random weight** (default 1); equal weights give equal chances. Order plays the displayed list top to bottom and repeats, ignoring weights. Reorder the action rows to change that sequence. Weights are retained when switching modes. Existing alternative enemies default to Random; classic enemies default to Order. Prepared reactions do not take an extra position in the normal action sequence. Their choice weights remain independent. Enemy action boxes use subtle colors for Attack, Defend, Prepare and Wait.

In the chapter editor, open **Library → Rat → Combat Actions → Prepare → Prepared actions**. Enemy Prepare selects one of the enemy’s own Combat Actions secretly, using relative weights. The selected action stays fixed across retries and expires after the next player action. The default entries are:

| Action | Weight | Trigger | Result |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Dodge (Defend) | 1 | Incoming player Attack hits, before damage | Its own check prevents the entire hit on success. Player preparation bypasses it for base damage. |
| Rush (Attack) | 1 | Player action finishes | Resolves its own damage profile and accuracy check; canceled if the enemy dies. |

Dodge references **Skip (Wait)** as its fallback after player Defend or Wait. Player Prepare and missed Attacks expire Dodge silently by default. The fallback can also be enabled after player Prepare, including a failed checked Prepare. Skip is not a third random selection unless you explicitly add it as one.

Edit these actions under **Library → Rat → Prepared actions**, including their names, descriptions, artwork, card types, checks, damage, and progression effects. The definitions belong to Rat and remain separate from its normal action rotation and from **Common**. Each Prepare action selects from this enemy-owned list. **Prepared actions** appears below **Combat Actions**. Use **+ Prepared Action** to create an Attack and open its editor; change its type to Defend or Wait in the action’s dropdown if needed. Creation does not assign an action to Prepare. Then open a Prepare under **Combat Actions**, use **Add choice**, and select an **Action**. **Trigger** follows the action picker and stays editable. Selecting an action sets a compatible trigger; changing the trigger clears an incompatible selection so you can choose a matching action. Equal chance weights give equal probabilities. Optional responses and their player-action checkboxes follow the trigger and weight. Enemy preparation requires empty costs and requirements and zero cooldown. Outcome effects are supported for checked actions. Selection timing and fallback belong to Prepare's reference, while the action owns its mechanics and presentation.

Existing alternative projects migrate on load without rewriting the original file until saved. Migration preserves the old cards as archival data and copies their authored presentation and independent checks into new enemy-owned definitions. Earlier shared prepared-action drafts also move into each owning enemy; a Common source used elsewhere is preserved as an independent source. Rush snapshots its former Attack damage plus the Prepare bonus; later edits to Bite or the archived Prepare bonus do not change Rush. Edit Rush's own damage profile instead. Player Prepare remains a separate next-action damage/defense-break bonus and is unchanged.

Combat uses one shared Action model and common damage, check, effect, artwork and description primitives. Ownership and execution rules remain specific to each side: player and Weapon editors keep the COMBAT V1 controls, costs, cooldowns and preparation behavior; enemy editors expose enemy selection, checked defense, presentation and fallback settings. Adding an enemy capability does not automatically add a player control or change player rules.

The pre-change project is committed as `612cabc` and tagged **COMBAT-V1** on origin.

Damage is committed when the corresponding effect card is swiped away. Missed attacks from either side, including rat charges, are communicated by the existing `MISS` check result and proceed directly to the next combat stage without a separate feedback card. Killing the rat cancels its pending charge. If the player defends or prepares against rat defense, the feedback is “The rat holds its ground”. The fixture omits health-threshold reactions to keep the turn rhythm clear.

All Actions in this example have zero cooldown. This does not change cooldown behavior for other projects. The alternative uses the shared coordinator and combat rules through the Enemy's explicit opt-in.

The rat's 8 Health and selection weights are provisional balance: Bite and Prepare each have a 50% chance; hidden defense and charge each have a 50% chance within Prepare. The next declaration is queued before each player choice and stays fixed across retries. A queued preparation becomes active only after its own preparation card is played.

Player preparation is free of Energy costs and dice rolls, but spends a turn. It applies only to the next player action: an Attack gains the authored bonus against an undefended target, or ignores defence and deals base damage only. Preparation is consumed on a miss, Defend, or Wait. A successful new Prepare replaces it rather than stacking; an optional checked Prepare that fails clears the old bonus. Incoming enemy actions do not consume it. The shared combat resolver applies the same breakthrough rule to numeric defence. Neither this update nor local project edits publish the mobile version.
