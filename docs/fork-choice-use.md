# Hide after use in Forks

Each native Fork choice has an optional **Hide after use** checkbox at the bottom of the card panel, aligned left beside **Duplicate**. It is off by default and belongs to that placement, not its Library definition. The **H** rule marker in the inspector and graph identifies enabled choices; its tooltip reads **Hide after use**.

A choice is used after an accepted, successfully resolved action. Both successful and failed dice checks count. Previewing, selecting, canceling a swipe or check, unmet requirements, unavailable routes and failed check feedback do not consume it. Effects and costs retain their normal transaction behavior.

Used choices disappear on return to that Fork for the rest of the run. Other placements of the same source remain available. New preview playback and a fresh restart reset the memory. Continuing into another chapter preserves run memory; restarting that chapter restores its entry snapshot.

An exhausted Fork remains a blocked destination using the existing unavailable-choice presentation. It does not automatically continue or disappear from Auto/Travel event pools. Authors may leave repeatable choices by keeping the checkbox off.

The placement setting participates in normal history, copy, duplication and project/chapter serialization. Moving or pasting the card outside Fork choices removes the Fork-only setting.
