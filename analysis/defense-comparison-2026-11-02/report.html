Rat combat balance: defense checks and Punch cost

2 November 2026. Frozen snapshot of rat-alternative.json. Rat 4 HP; player 10 HP; Agility 3; initial Energy 3. A separate sensitivity run starts at 10 Energy.

Rules compared: checked defense uses the engine’s 3d6 ≥ 10 reaction roll (62.5% block chance after a hit); guaranteed defense blocks the hit without a defense roll, still costs 1 Energy and uses the same action/guard timing. Rat defense and attack dice are unchanged. Paid Punch costs 1 Energy; free Punch costs 0. Punch deals 1–2 base damage; free Prepare adds 2 and breaks a rat block (the preparation bonus is absorbed by breaking the block).

Main finding at the current 3 Energy start: repeated Punch rises from 24.70% wins to 89.05% when Punch is free. Defend-on-tell with free Punch rises from 87.47% with checked defense to 94.34% with guaranteed defense. The best observed paid-Punch strategy wins 74.95% under either defense rule. Recommendation: try free Punch plus guaranteed paid defense if the goal is reliable, understandable defense; expect a forgiving first encounter and retune rat difficulty separately if that is too easy.

How to read this

Defense competes directly with Punch for the starting Energy budget. Guaranteed defense improves protection when chosen, but cannot by itself repair a strategy that spends the Energy needed to kill the rat. Free Punch removes that failure mode and makes defense spending less punishing. Preparing remains a strong offensive option. These are strategy-specific encounter outcomes, not a measurement of campaign balance or proof that one strategy is optimal.

Method and verification

10,000 seeded headless production-coordinator encounters per cell; 7 strategies × 4 variants × 2 Energy starts = 560,000 retained encounters. An independent code review caught and corrected the random policy so it selects uniformly from legal actions, including free Punch at zero Energy. No-defense policy results are asserted identical across the defense variants. Unaffordable actions fall back to the free Prepare action until the encounter resolves; there is no Energy regeneration. Player choices see HP, rat HP, Energy, preparation, guard and the visible rat preparation cue; they never see its hidden block/charge branch. Shared seeds reproduce each cell but removing rolls changes later random-number alignment, so comparisons are not paired identical trajectories. Win intervals describe sampling uncertainty, not model uncertainty. Winner HP and Energy are conditional on victory.

37 relevant existing engine tests passed. Physical dice, browser presentation, animation and devices were not tested. The original project and production engine were not edited. The guaranteed-defense variant exists only as an isolated analysis override.
